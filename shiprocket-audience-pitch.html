02

Interest ≠ intent

Platforms target people who scrolled past a product. Shiprocket knows people who opened their wallets. That's a fundamentally different signal.

03

Tier 2+ is a blind spot

45%+ of India's online GMV comes from Tier 2+ cities — exactly where most platforms are weakest and Shiprocket's data is deepest.

04

Signal loss keeps accelerating

iOS restrictions and cookie deprecation keep degrading third-party data. First-party purchase signals are the only durable answer.

Beyond D2C

The data doesn't
care what you sell.

Every buyer in our graph is also a telecom subscriber, a potential hotel guest, a credit card applicant. Shiprocket's purchase signals are cross-sector by nature — the same verified transaction that tells a D2C brand who to target tells a telco who to upgrade.

📡 Telecom
ARPU segmentation without survey data

Airtel, Vodafone, Jio

The signal

Every buyer who transacts online is a smartphone user. Their AOV is a better proxy for disposable income than any declared survey. Geography tells you which pin codes are under-penetrated on postpaid.

Sample brief

Electronics buyers in Tier 2/3 pin codes, AOV ₹5,000+, last 6 months — for postpaid upgrade push.

"If someone bought a ₹15K phone in Kanpur, they're exactly who Airtel wants to upgrade — and Meta would never find them."

🏨 Hotels & Travel
Purchase patterns reveal travel intent

Marriott, Taj, Lemon Tree, OYO

The signal

Luggage, travel accessories, formal wear ordered from non-home pin codes — all in our graph. High-frequency metro buyers who've shipped to 3+ cities in a year are almost certainly business travelers.

Sample brief

Travel accessories buyers + 4+ orders/year + Metro delivery pin codes — for loyalty programme enrolment.

"Someone who's shipped to Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Delhi this year is a traveler. We know that. No booking platform does."

💳 BFSI
Real spend as a credit signal

The signal

Verified purchase frequency and AOV is a declared spending pattern — far more reliable than modelled income estimates. Prepaid payment buyers with rising AOVs are the credit-hungry, credit-worthy cohort every card issuer wants.

Sample brief
